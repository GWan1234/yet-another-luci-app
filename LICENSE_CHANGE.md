# License Notice & Relicensing Explanation

Effective as of August 2026, **Yet Another LuCI App** has transitioned its open-source license from GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3) to the **Apache License 2.0**.

---

## Why the License Changed

The shift to Apache-2.0 establishes a sustainable **open-core distribution model** that serves both the homelab/FOSS community and the official Play Store release:

1. **Permissive & Unrestricted Community Builds**:
   - The Apache 2.0 license grants full legal freedom for individuals, organizations, and open-source contributors to fork, modify, embed, or distribute the application without copyleft restrictions.
   - The official **Community Build** (published directly on GitHub Releases) remains 100% free, unlimited, ad-free, and open-source.

2. **Dual-Distribution Architecture**:
   - Apache-2.0 allows a funded **Play Store Edition** (incorporating optional subscriptions, Google Play Billing, and AdMob support to cover infrastructure and ongoing development costs) to coexist alongside the Community Build cleanly in good conscience.

3. **Forward-Looking Scope**:
   - This relicensing applies to all code, releases, and contributions going forward from version `1.0.0+12`.
   - Historical release tags prior to this transition remain governed by their respective original licensing at the time of release.

## Summary of Dual Builds

| Feature / Capability | Community Build (GitHub Releases) | Play Store Build (Google Play) |
|---|---|---|
| **License** | Apache 2.0 (Open Source) | Commercial / Distributed via Play Store |
| **OpenWrt Router Limit** | **Unlimited** | Tier-Gated (Free: 1, Plus: 3, Pro: Unlimited) |
| **Advertisements** | **None (100% Ad-Free)** | Banner Ads on Free Tier |
| **In-App Purchases** | **None (SDK Stripped)** | Google Play Billing Enabled |
| **Source Code Availability** | 100% Open Source | Open Core + Closed Monetization Layer |
